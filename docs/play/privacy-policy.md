# TilePDF Privacy Policy

*Effective 19 October 2026*

TilePDF is a PDF reader for Android. It is built to work entirely on your
device.

## No data leaves your device

- TilePDF has **no internet access**. The app doesn't request Android's
  internet permission, so it can't send or receive anything over the network.
- TilePDF contains **no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or account
  system**.
- The developer **does not collect, store or share** any personal data or
  usage information.
- **Text recognition** on scanned pages runs on your device with Google ML
  Kit's built-in model. Your pages are never sent anywhere. ML Kit is made
  to report usage statistics to Google, but without internet access it
  can't.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything below stays in the app's private storage on your device:

- **Documents you open.** TilePDF reads them through Android's file picker or
  when another app shares them. Some files are copied into private storage
  so they can be reopened later.
- **Annotations.** Highlights, drawings and notes are saved into the PDF file
  itself, as is recognised text. Until they are saved, a draft stays in the
  app's private database.
- **Library.** The list of documents you opened, reading positions, favorites
  and bookmarks.
- **Preferences.** Reading settings, theme and language.

You can delete all of it by uninstalling TilePDF, or with *Settings → Apps →
TilePDF → Clear storage*. Removing a document from the library also deletes
its private copy and unsaved annotations.

## Android backup

If Android backup is on, Android may include TilePDF's **preferences** in your
own device backup. Your documents, annotations and library are not included.
This backup is managed by Android and your Google account, and the developer
cannot access it.

## Permissions

TilePDF asks for no runtime permissions. The few install-time permissions in
the app come from Android libraries that save annotations in the background.
None of them gives access to the network or to your personal data.

## Children

TilePDF doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new
effective date.
