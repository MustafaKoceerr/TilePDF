TilePDF is a fast PDF reader that keeps everything on your phone. It has no internet permission, no ads and no account, so your documents never leave your device.

READ COMFORTABLY
• Smooth scrolling and sharp zoom, even on large documents
• Vertical scrolling or one page at a time
• Light, night and sepia page colors
• Search the whole document and jump between results
• Table of contents, bookmarks and a page grid
• Tap a page for full-screen reading
• Reopens every document exactly where you left it
• Opens password-protected PDFs

MARK UP YOUR PDFS
• Highlight, underline or strike out text
• Draw with a pen in several colors and widths, and erase strokes
• Add sticky notes
• Undo and redo
• Annotations are saved into the PDF itself, so other PDF apps show them too
• Files that can't be changed are saved as an annotated copy

YOUR LIBRARY
• Every document you opened, ready to continue
• Favorites, sorting and search
• Open PDFs from any app with "Open with" or "Share"

MADE FOR EVERY SCREEN
• Tablets and foldables keep the contents in a side panel next to the pages
• Light, dark or system theme, with optional Material You colors
• English and Turkish
• TalkBack labels and large touch targets

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
TilePDF works completely offline. It collects no data and shares nothing. Your files, notes and settings stay on your device.

TilePDF uses the PDFium engine, the same engine that renders PDFs in Chrome.
