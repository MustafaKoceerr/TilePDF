TilePDF is a fast PDF reader that keeps everything on your phone. No ads, no account, and your documents never leave your device.

READ COMFORTABLY
• Smooth scrolling and pinch zoom that follows your fingers, even on large documents
• Vertical scrolling or one page at a time
• Six page colors: light, sepia, green, and for the dark dim, warm night and night, all easy on the eyes
• Search the whole document and jump between results
• Table of contents, bookmarks and a page grid, with a way back after every jump
• Rotate pages and crop empty margins
• Turn pages with the volume keys
• Tap a page for full-screen reading
• Reopens every document exactly where you left it
• Opens password-protected PDFs

MARK UP, FILL IN AND SIGN
• Highlight, underline or strike out text; tap again to take it off
• Draw with a pen in several colors and widths, and erase strokes
• Add sticky notes
• Fill in PDF forms: text fields, check boxes, radio buttons and lists; totals calculate themselves
• Sign with your finger; your signature is kept for next time
• Check a document's digital signatures
• Recognise text on scanned pages to search and copy it, right on your phone
• Undo and redo
• Annotations are saved into the PDF itself, so other PDF apps show them too
• Files that can't be changed are saved as an annotated copy
• Share or print a document with its annotations and filled-in forms

YOUR LIBRARY
• Every document you opened, ready to continue, with its first page as a cover
• Favorites, sorting and search
• Open PDFs from any app with "Open with" or "Share"

MADE FOR EVERY SCREEN
• Tablets and foldables keep the contents in a side panel next to the pages
• Two pages side by side, like a book, on wide screens
• Light, dark or system theme in violet, ocean, forest or sand, or Material You colors
• English and Turkish
• TalkBack labels and large touch targets

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
TilePDF works without an internet connection. Your files, notes and settings stay on your device; their contents are never sent anywhere. TilePDF only sends crash reports, which you can turn off, and anonymous usage statistics if you allow them.

TilePDF uses the PDFium engine, the same engine that renders PDFs in Chrome.
